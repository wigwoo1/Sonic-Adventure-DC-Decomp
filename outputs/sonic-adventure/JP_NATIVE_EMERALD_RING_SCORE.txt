Emerald Coast ring and life accounting on Windows

A confirmed stationary ring pickup now calls the pinned HUD ring
accounting code and updates the existing game ring and life variables.
The generated action-stage build uses the original hundred-ring rule
and life cap behavior. Field-specific display tasks and the extra-life
sound are outside this build.

Build with build_native_emerald_ring_score.py using the pinned checkout
and the ring contact baseline. Run verify_native_emerald_ring_score.py
with the local disc and scene inputs. The scheduler test starts at 99
rings and three lives, then checks 100 rings and four lives after one
pickup. Repeated ticks and falling contact award no further rings.

The test supplies the collector sphere and reads the variables directly.
Visible HUD, live player collision integration, audio, and ring sparkle
rendering remain open. Overall project completion is about 10%.
