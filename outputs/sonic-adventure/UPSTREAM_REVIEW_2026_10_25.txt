1. consolesplayingconsoles/sonic-adventure-decomp
Reviewed README at commit a06f0c01d97f676cb673b4bfffe6f26e6a69f07b:
https://github.com/consolesplayingconsoles/sonic-adventure-decomp/blob/a06f0c01d97f676cb673b4bfffe6f26e6a69f07b/README.md
It targets the European MK-5100050 V1.003 executable. Its README reports an
assembly rebuild baseline, 5179 executable functions, 1088 named functions and
zero equivalent/matching C functions. Names are not decompilation completion.
Its executable is 6735296 bytes, SHA1 7ca46c7d78f9bd869fdf5d8b6fa93da516dffded.
The user's local USA executable is 6735488 bytes, SHA1
e74c849806db103aa87f84e41a382873bc96b6df (SHA256 remains the pinned 45bc5d3c...).
Those are different inputs. Its single-executable count is not a measured
whole-game USA denominator, including overlays and other modules. Its symbol/
structure/tooling approach may inform later work, but cross-version addresses
or names require local evidence. Its build description also requires a Katana
SDK; no such material was obtained for this review.
