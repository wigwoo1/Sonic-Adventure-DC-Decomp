Observed behavior
The material loop selects this pair for layout 2200000 or 3200000 hex (with
secondary-header bit 100000 handled separately). The segment's +14 hex field
points to signed 16-bit U/V pairs. Each coordinate converts to float and scales
by 1/256. The helper writes factor 1.0 on every emitted ordinary vertex, including
the first two. It does not calculate normal lighting or reuse earlier factor
words. Output packets contain control, projected x/y/reciprocal depth, U/V,
factor 1.0 and an untouched eighth word. Triangle, quad and strip loops retain
original winding/rejection, UV advancement, counters and cursor behavior.

Near crossings stage vertex pointers and six raw signed UV words to lower
helper 8c6124c0. Alternate topology c000 delegates to 8c61430e / 8c616068 with
the parent's original parameter/bank preparation. All three lower bodies remain
explicit fixtures in this checkpoint; their output beyond those calls is not
yet proved. No numerical normalizer executes in these tests.
