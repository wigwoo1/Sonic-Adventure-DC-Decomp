Observed behavior
The UV-constant parent delegates alternate topology c000 to these helpers. Strip
metadata controls length, winding and restart groups. The helpers consume signed
16-bit U/V words, convert to floats and scale by 1/256. The ordinary emitter
receives its UV scale through inactive-bank FR0, as staged by its parent.

Every emitted vertex receives factor 1.0. There is no normal lighting calculation,
normalizer call, factor-cache write or cache write-back in these two bodies.
Packets contain control, projected x/y/reciprocal depth, U/V, factor and an
untouched final word. The clipped helper stages vertex pointers and signed UVs
to native clipper 8c6124c0 for accepted near crossings. Quantized clipped output
retains factor 1.0 on every vertex; quadrilaterals use strip order 0,1,3,2.
Existing empty stub 8c611708 and constant-factor UV clipper are reused without
additional recovery credit. The complete parent/clipper/alternate chain is native
for tested inputs; it still does not produce visible GPU output.
