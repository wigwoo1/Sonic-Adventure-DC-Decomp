Original collision registration on Windows

The pinned collision_register, collision_reset, and collision_clear_lists
bodies now compile into the Emerald Coast Frame DLL with their original
list storage and limits. Registration observes the shared frame_redrawing
flag and the original edit-mode flag. The build also preserves the two
list counters originally defined in the stage source.

Build with build_native_emerald_collision_lists.py using the pinned
checkout and the qualified Emerald gameplay clock build. The exported
jp_collision_lists_check returns zero on success when called in a fresh
process with the new DLL. It passed twice across all nine collision kinds,
including capacity, reset, clear, redraw, and editor cases. The Emerald
Coast scene passed 857 regression checks and matched all eight prior
captures. Scene collision behavior still runs through the prior path.

Pair dispatch, mesh updates, and switching the live stage to the original
collision pipeline remain open. Overall completion is about 10 percent.
The generated game source and combined DLL stay local.
