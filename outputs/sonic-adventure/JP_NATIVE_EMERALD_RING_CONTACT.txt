Stationary ring contact on Windows

The placed ring task now checks contact against a supplied Sonic sphere.
The Windows adapter uses the original strict sphere overlap condition
and collision reset routine. The next scheduler tick consumes a hit,
moves the ring into sparkle mode, and emits one award unless Sonic is
falling.

Build with build_native_emerald_ring_contact.py and the qualified ring
initialization build. Run verify_native_emerald_ring_contact.py using the
same local disc and scene inputs. The test covers tangent rejection,
next-tick pickup, duplicate suppression and the falling case.

The fixture supplies the sphere and award receiver. Full collision
registration, HUD and audio, sparkle rendering and completion, and
moving-ring behavior remain open. Overall completion is about 10%.
