Original file-read behavior
Command11 receives channel, filename, caller output and requested block count
through the original argument/opcode APIs and scheduler. A zero requested count
queries the file's decoded block count; nonzero count bypasses that query. Open
checks original admission/readiness/root/name/FAT rules and allocates the first
free entry in the original eight-slot handle pool. The handle stores remaining
blocks and the current FAT coordinate. Successful reads copy512 bytes and follow
the original FAT link. EOF returns zero without touching the remaining caller
buffer, even when the requested count is larger than the file. The master still
finishes successfully in that case. It closes after successful open, preserves
a negative read result and ignores the close return. These original rules are
preserved without adding range repair, invented I/O or stronger save guarantees.
