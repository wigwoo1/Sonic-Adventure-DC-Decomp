Original Sonic input intent on Windows

The native frame build now links the pinned player stick reader and
Sonic walking-action selector. The test supplies controlled controller
samples through the Windows input backend and runs the original pad
scan before the player reads its stick.

Nine cases cover neutral and opposed stick input, disabled input,
launch suppression, the path exception, event-driven input, and the
Casinopolis ball fallback. At a prepared walking speed, the original
selector chooses the walk action and sets its animation pace.
The scheduled animation and GPU regression passed 457 checks.
