Emerald Coast act-one texture and material verification

The verifier reads the original STG01 and BEACH01 archives directly
from the supplied Japanese disc. It checks that the stage texture-list
names agree with the archive's numbered slots, then walks the drawable
land models to validate material indices, point indices, and UV extents.

The Windows texture owner loaded and released all 97 archive slots.
The check compared 1,403,158 encoded payload bytes, accounting for the
four-byte prefix the native owner adds to 16-bit mip chains. The stage
references 69 slots across 951 textured meshsets and 37,352 UV corners.

Run verify_native_emerald_materials.py against the qualified Emerald
task build, pinned checkout and local Japanese track. The verifier
qualifies texture ownership and stage bindings. Applying the original
UVs and materials in the GPU scene remains open. The archive and pixel
data stay local. Overall project completion is about 10%.
