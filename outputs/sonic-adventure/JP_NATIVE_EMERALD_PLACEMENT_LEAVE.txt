Emerald Coast placement range retirement on Windows

The Windows placement runtime now links the pinned game's two
placement_try_leave functions. The adapter snapshots camera and player
positions for the task phase and preserves the redraw guard. The
original policy schedules a task for destruction on its next tick.
That destruction clears its live flag so its placement can spawn again.

Build with build_native_emerald_placement_leave.py against the pinned
checkout and qualified Emerald Coast kind-table build. Run
verify_native_emerald_placement_leave.py with the local disc and scene
inputs. Thirteen tasks survived when near the camera or player, during
redraw, or with unlimited range. All thirteen retired on the next
scheduler tick when far away and respawned on returning.

The fixture calls the leave functions on real scheduler tasks with
diagnostic callbacks. The original ring callback, collision,
collection and drawing remain open. Overall completion is about 10%.
