Behavior
The helpers emit strips selected by topology c000. They handle alternating
winding, the input flip bit, strict orientation rejection, packet grouping,
original counters and cursor advancement. Zero-area triangles are rejected.

For ordinary output they use FTRV on the normal plus homogeneous 1, with the
other-bank matrix prepared by the parent. Its first two outputs are UVs and its
third is the lighting factor. The parent scales the normal-mapping columns by
0.5, sets U/V translation to 0.5, supplies negative light-vector coefficients
and sets the lighting base. Existing register values and FPU-bank effects are
preserved. UVs are floating point here, without the near-helper's integer
quantization or a clamp to 0..1.

Packets contain control, projected x/y/reciprocal depth, U/V and lighting factor.
The eighth queue word retains its prior value. Ordinary paths leave the input
factor-cache word unchanged and issue no OCBWB write-back event. Clipped paths
check shared rejection flags and near crossings; after orientation acceptance,
crossings call recovered environment clipper 8c611f24 with orientation disabled.
That clipper reads the matrix through 8c890238 and performs both original integer
UV quantization steps. Its four-vertex output uses strip order 0,1,3,2.
The existing empty stub at 8c611708 and native clipper are reused without new
recovery credit. Both new bodies call the stub; the clipped body also calls the
clipper. All three sites execute natively in the focused tests.
