Scheduled Sonic jump dispatch in Emerald Coast

The native level movement dispatcher reads the sampled jump edge,
launches Sonic from rest, and routes subsequent scheduler ticks to
the neutral airborne step until he lands. Ground movement uses the
verified standing, walking and stopping paths.

Build with build_native_emerald_jump_dispatch.py using the verified
vertical-jump build. Run verify_native_emerald_jump_dispatch.py with
the same local disc, collision and character inputs. The Windows
build compiled, linked and passed 600 scene checks. The probe injects
one sampled button edge after the pickup recording, then checks
launch, ascent, descent and landing through the scheduled callback.
The screenshots show the earlier pickup.

Physical controller jump input and airborne animation playback remain
unverified. Overall project completion is about 10%.
