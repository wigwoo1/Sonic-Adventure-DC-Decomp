Native Sonic ground jump launch

The launch helper accepts the sampled jump press in ordinary standing
or walking on static ground. It selects jump mode and the spin action,
applies Sonic's launch speed, and resets the jump counter.

Build with build_native_emerald_ground_jump.py using the verified
level-stop build. Run verify_native_emerald_ground_jump.py with the
same local disc, collision and character inputs. The Windows build
compiled, linked and passed 600 scene checks. The probe separately
checks no press, a sampled press and an attempted second launch.
Both captures show the earlier ring pickup.

The test invokes the helper after the stop sequence and injects the
sampled press into the pad record. Normal mode dispatch, launch sound,
air movement and landing remain integration work. Overall completion
is about 10%.
