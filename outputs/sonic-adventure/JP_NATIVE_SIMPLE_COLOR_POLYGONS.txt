Native Ninja Simple unlit quads and N-gons

The bounded model dispatcher now supports untextured, unlit quads
and N-gons. It validates the whole index span and reserves up to four
output vertices for each constituent triangle. The clipped quad keeps
the original shared winding decision between its two triangles. The
N-gon path uses the original strip order.

Build with build_native_simple_color_polygons.py against the pinned
checkout and jp-native-simple-env-strips-v1. Run
verify_native_simple_color_polygons.py against the result. Seven cases
check opaque and translucent packets, clipping, and malformed spans.
Nine environment-strip cases, two mixed-model cases, and all 3,341
projection checks passed. Verification covers CPU queue output.
Textured and lit polygon paths and GPU presentation remain open.
Overall project completion is about 10%.
