Observed behavior
The environment-face parent delegates alternate topology c000 to these helpers.
Strip metadata controls lengths, winding and output restarts. Ordinary accepted
vertices use FTRV with a prepared inactive-bank matrix: matrix columns 0/1 are
scaled for environment UVs, offsets are 0.5, the lighting column contains negative
light coefficients and its offset contains ambient intensity. Ordinary factors
are per-vertex; input caches and the final packet word remain unchanged.

For accepted near-plane crossings, the clipped helper sums three normals, calls
existing native normalizer 8c63a94c and calculates one face-lighting factor. It
stages vertex pointers and active FR4 to native clipper 8c6173ba, which generates
signed integer UVs through the separately published matrix, interpolates and
quantizes them again, and emits the clipped polygon. The first two emitted
vertices retain earlier queue factors; the third and, for quadrilaterals, fourth
copy the supplied face factor. Padding remains untouched. Four-vertex output
uses strip order 0,1,3,2.
Ordinary prepared-register UVs and crossing published-memory UVs are checked as
separate data paths. Existing empty stub, normalizer and clipper are reused without
additional routine credit. The tested parent/helper chain is native; visible GPU
output remains unfinished.
