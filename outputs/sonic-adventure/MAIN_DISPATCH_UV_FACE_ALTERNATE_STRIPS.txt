Observed behavior
The UV-face parent delegates alternate topology c000 to these helpers. Strip
metadata controls length, winding and restart groups. The helpers consume signed
16-bit UV pairs, convert to floats and scale by 1/256. Ordinary accepted vertices
receive per-vertex normal/light dot-product factors, without a factor-cache write
or OCBWB. Output is control, projected x/y/reciprocal depth, U/V, factor and an
untouched final word. Factors are per-vertex on this path despite the parent
family's face lighting behavior for ordinary non-alternate triangles.

For accepted near-plane crossings, the clipped helper sums three vertex normals,
calls existing native normalizer 8c63a94c and computes a single face factor. Raw
signed UVs and pointers are staged to native clipper 8c616f18 with the factor in
active FR4. The clipper interpolates/quantizes UVs before 1/256 scaling. The first
two emitted vertices retain the selected queue's earlier factor; the third and,
for quadrilaterals, fourth receive the supplied face factor. Final packet words
remain untouched. Crossing quadrilaterals use strip order 0,1,3,2.
Existing empty stub 8c611708, normalizer and clipper are reused without new credit.
