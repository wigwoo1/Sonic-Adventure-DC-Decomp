Native storage and bus behavior

ta_stream.c implements the portable owned stream and allocator interface.
ta_stream_win32.c supplies native VirtualAlloc/VirtualFree allocation for this
Windows research build. No Python callback supplies allocations during the
original-game comparisons. Allocation callbacks in the separate fault tests
only exercise host-memory failures; they never provide game/device results.

Accepted SQ packets retain their original destination, queue index and eight
words. DMA FIFO words are assembled in order and become visible only as complete
packets. Original prefetch/flush counters and ordered trace effects remain
checked. Non-command accesses use the existing parent bus. The stream is bound
below channel-2 DMA so its writes cannot bypass the new capture endpoint.

List events keep their original header, vertex, EOL, pending and acknowledgment
rules. Guest list-init strobes retire the active page chain into separate owned
storage and start a new generation. Explicit storage snapshots make independent
deep copies. Later append operations, list retirement and subsequent retirement
cannot change an existing snapshot. A failed snapshot releases its temporary
pages and leaves the previous snapshot and submission cursor intact.

Limits and next work

The new paged capture comparison explicitly rejects pixel-job seeds and restore
of a nonempty retired generation until those integrations have their own full
verification. It does not claim a completed frame from the 186-packet stream.
The generic native snapshot API has ownership tests, but connecting snapshots
to original render-start strobes and completed pixel jobs remains to be done.
The bounded preview's two completed jobs do not qualify that integration.

Next connect immutable page snapshots to the original render-start path and
native pixel jobs. Preserve all packet ordering, header/strip continuity and
list-generation ownership, and compare the complete resulting colour/depth
images against the independent oracle. Then continue original draw 8c01bd9e
and later camera updates. Do not substitute completion tokens, drop packets,
resume faults or claim that raising a constant qualifies complete rendering.

Continue Sol High for that renderer and reverse-engineering work. Luna Low is
suitable for routine publishing. Registry counts remain 1,658 entries, 1,655

60/120/240 FPS, 8K performance, SUPIR/settings, Android, stereoscopic 3DS and
later N64/PS1 ports remain unfinished. Texture Off must bypass sampling and
retain vertex/material colours.
