Sonic Adventure USA Rev A: movement record adjustment, 2026-10-28

What was recovered
07506E applies a selected movement record to the actor and query position.
It follows the state+6C pointer, then that object's +28 record pointer. A null
record returns immediately. A nonzero actor byte at +8 selects the second
28-byte record. Zero movement length first forms actor position + displacement.

When the selected record has a callback, the routine saves actor position,
temporarily installs the query position, and calls through the live pointer.
The callback receives the character pointer selected by the actor byte, the
actor pointer, and the selected record. After returning, the routine restores
actor position, reloads the record's live translation, adds it to the query,
and applies three angle deltas modulo 65536. Callback effects outside the saved
position remain. A missing callback body stays a reached unresolved target.

0751C0 adds three times an input impulse to an actor position, then adds twice
the X/Z impulse to the query's X/Z coordinates. Query Y is unchanged. The factor
three is read from the original constant at 0753A0; the original instructions
form the factor two. Both bodies preserve original instruction/operation order.
