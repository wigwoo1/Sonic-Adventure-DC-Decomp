Verification
226 focused original/native comparisons / 2,433 independent checks cover list
masks, all five format overrides, compactness/order selection, opaque controls,
wrapping size arithmetic, address boundaries and reused linked-list/global effects.
Independent mathematical/graph oracles supplement whole CPU/FPU/RAM/VRAM/Sound,
device and ordered-call-boundary comparisons. Direct cases use declared input
configuration data and valid mapped aligned caller storage. Physical misaligned
address exceptions, arbitrary callbacks/concurrency and actual pixels are outside
this qualification. No guest checkpoint or helper result is supplied.

All 2,245 earlier direct and retained comparisons execute freshly against the new
DLL: transfer dispatch/handler/context/CPU, runtime/request, legacy ABI, packet/
flash, audio mode/controls, MIDI packing and command sender. Historical evidence
files remain unchanged. A preliminary replay wrapper selected the older DLL for
some tests; the compatibility-export check caught it. The import was corrected,
an explicit new-DLL hash check added, and the discarded replay rerun. It supplies
no qualification credit. The separate original model and native DLL are unchanged.

Two completed fresh configured startup comparisons begin at original 0x8c0546c0.
Original platform initialization, flash parsing/CRC, MIDI producer, allocation and
graphics setup execute, and startup now reaches missing routine 0x8c608d88 after
0x8c656940 returns. All previous 200-source boundary fields match through entry
to 0x8c656940; the new read-only descriptor observation adds no state. Explicit raw
flash/platform data remain declared inputs; these runs are not reset boot proof.
Cold startup retains the full 8 MiB video clear, two complete 2 MiB Sound fills,
two 33,376-byte transfers and original IRQ/RTE/REQUEST returns. All 1,065,282 Sound
events compare without drops. All 39 pending audio queue records and 32 MIDI
commands retain their independent byte checks. Pending commands do not establish
ARM consumption, audible output, hardware timing, visible rendering or gameplay.
Total qualified/replayed comparisons: 2,473, including both startup profiles.
Graphics setup writes 3,600 tile-descriptor words after the complete clear. The
earlier verifier's final-all-zero VRAM expectation therefore became obsolete.
It now retains full-clear instruction/count/error checks and replays all later
ordinary writes over the checked zero clear to reproduce the final 8 MiB hash.
This is a consistency check alongside full original/native memory comparison;
it does not qualify the entire layout builder or establish rendered pixels.
