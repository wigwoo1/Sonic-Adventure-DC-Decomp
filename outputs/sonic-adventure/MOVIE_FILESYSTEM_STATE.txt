What changed

retrying reader at 647610. It requests and receives two 256-byte responses,
assembles the actual 512 bytes at the original destination, updates the original
statistics, calls the real progress callback, and resumes its caller. Execution
then reaches the real missing root-block validator at 64a4b2. It remains pending
there; no successful parser result or completion callback is fabricated.

Two routines qualify: gateway 64ae32 has 12 original instructions and no branches;
retry reader 647610 has 90 instructions and 14 conditional outcomes. Every one of
these 102 rows and 14 outcomes executed in tests. Registry: 1,515 entries / 1,512
unique module-address pairs. The previous 1,513 registry rows are preserved.

The new source also translates the 44-instruction table reader at 64ae62. Its
nonpositive-count path has independent tests, but its positive loop and error
returns remain unqualified. It is NOT registered and receives no routine credit.
The full filesystem and the separately identified write backend remain unfinished.

Continuation and preservation limits

The source adds three nonfunction continuation exports: 647646, 647694 and
64ae9a. Of these, 647646 is qualified through actual task resumption. The existing
caller continuation 64b21e is also newly qualified. That makes 13 qualified and
72 unqualified routes out of 85 total. Exports 647694 and 64ae9a remain unqualified
for nonlocal resumption; none receives function credit.

The old runtime, ABI, exception/context code and guard policies are preserved.
The bridge adds three bodies, three routes, the export prefix and exactly three
original call-return sites: its allowed membership grows from 1,971 to 1,974.
This does not allow arbitrary returns. The original CPU and flat execution code
are unchanged. Static audits check all 146 proposed instruction rows against
the retail bytes. Generation alone gives no recovery credit.

Physical Maple DMA traversal, acquisition, timing and automatic completion
interrupts remain unfinished. Busy-clear and raw RX bytes are declared device

unfinished, as do FPS/8K/SUPIR/settings. Texture Off means disabling texture
