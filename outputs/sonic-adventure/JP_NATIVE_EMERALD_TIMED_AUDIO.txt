Emerald Coast audio mixed alongside scene frames

The verifier services the native effect queue and renders one PCM block
per streamed scene frame, plus a block after landing. It budgets stereo
samples at 44.1 kHz against the 60000/1001 logical tick rate.

Run verify_native_emerald_timed_audio.py with the verified jump-audio
build and the same local disc, collision and character inputs. The run
passed 816 checks and rendered 36,051 sample frames over 49 blocks.
The native slots reached the mixer in pickup, jump and landing order
at zero-based blocks 3, 11 and 48. The WAV stays local.

The harness records PCM during the run. Windows audio-device playback
and measured output latency remain unverified. Overall completion
remains about 10%.
