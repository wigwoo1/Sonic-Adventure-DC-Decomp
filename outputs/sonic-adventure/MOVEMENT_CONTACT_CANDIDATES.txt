What these routines do
Both routines scan the contact list using the count in query+28 and58-byte
records at6bacf0. They compare actual distances, angles and dot products.
075686 selects an eligible minimum distance and clamps query motion to it.

adds response delta, copies the selected normal and computes an intersection
of planes. It chooses the direction, preserves auxiliary speed, writes velocity
and copies that velocity into response delta. The completed redirect returns12.
Earlier returns are zero, and some occur after writes; these writes are retained.
