Emerald Coast native draw component timing

The instrumented build records separate wall-clock times for the Sonic
draw, terrain draw, ring and sparkle draw, and frame retention. The
verifier checks their sum against the enclosing native frame time.

Build with build_native_emerald_scene_profile.py from the verified
recording-profile build. Run verify_native_emerald_scene_profile.py
with the same local disc, collision and character inputs. The build
compiled, linked and passed 840 checks with 48 streamed frames.
Terrain took 11.06 seconds of the 12.28 seconds in the native draw
and submit call. Sonic took 334 ms, rings and sparkles 875 ms, and
frame retention about 2 ms in total. Audio drained without cancellation.

The next profile should split terrain visibility, mesh conversion and
packet emission. Overall project completion remains about 10%.
