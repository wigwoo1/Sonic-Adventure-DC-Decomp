Native Ninja Simple unlit textured triangles (Windows research)

The new jp_simple_draw_unlit_tex_triangles entry joins the pinned original
unlit textured triangle branch to the existing projection lease, texture
selector and retained queue. It validates the UV span, skips the UVs of
culled triangles, and writes signed UVs in units of 1/256. Opaque and
translucent paths use their original textured intensity headers.

Run build_native_simple_unlit_tex.py with the pinned upstream checkout
and jp-native-simple-unlit-strips-v2, then run
verify_native_simple_unlit_tex.py against the result. Four cases check
opaque and translucent packet bytes, short UVs, and a missing texture.
Both submitted frames retain their exact synthetic texture bytes after
the source texture list is released. Eight strip and six fixed-triangle
regressions pass, along with 3,341 original projection comparisons.

The new path covers one unlit textured triangle meshset without partial
clipping. Textured clipping, textured strips, lit materials and complete
model dispatch remain open. No new GPU presentation or direct execution
comparison against the original SH-4 textured drawer was performed.
Generated upstream code and the combined DLL stay local.

Overall project estimate: approximately 10%.
