Isolated Sonic jump and landing audio

After verifying the scene's three queued sound events, the test stops
the sound slots and lets the firmware settle. It then requests the
jump and landing effects individually. Each rendered 44,160 stereo
PCM frames at 44.1 kHz. Both outputs contain sound and their hashes
differ. The test retains the WAV files locally.

Run verify_native_emerald_isolated_jump_audio.py with the verified
jump-audio build and the same local disc, collision and character
inputs. The run passed 814 scene checks and the isolated PCM checks.

is about 10%.
