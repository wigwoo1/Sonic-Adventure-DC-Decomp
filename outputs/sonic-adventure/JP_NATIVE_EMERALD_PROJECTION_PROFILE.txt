Projection timing in Emerald Coast

The Windows projection diagnostic compiled, linked and passed 849 checks
over a run with 48 streamed frames. The verifier reads and resets nested
timers on each callback. At 781,250 wall counts per second, the projection
loop took 11.27 seconds out of 11.56 seconds inside the original model
calculation, about 97.5 percent. The difference includes other work in
the original calculation and timer overhead. The bounded drawer timer has
a separate scope and includes record handling. Both sets of counters can
include draws between streamed frames.

The authored builder and verifier are public. Their inputs, generated
game code, diagnostic DLL and screenshots remain local. This is a
performance diagnostic. Overall completion remains about 10 percent.
