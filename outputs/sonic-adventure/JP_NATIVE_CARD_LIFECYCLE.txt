startup must validate real setup and must not infer success from card_init's
return. Unsupported/corrupt imported cards, original ignored metadata-write
errors and whole-operation crash recovery remain the limitations recorded in
JP_NATIVE_VMU_FILESYSTEM.txt. Do not use these research tests as a user-save
migration tool. The original game-level save UI/write/read helper family has
not all been ported merely by testing the underlying record/container round trip.
