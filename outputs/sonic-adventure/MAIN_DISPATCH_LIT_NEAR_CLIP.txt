Behavior
For three input cache records, this helper first computes the same lighting
factor as the parent primitive family:
  factor = ((base - normal.x * light.x) - normal.y * light.y)
           - normal.z * light.z
It saves each factor at cache+1c before orientation rejection, including when
the triangle is rejected. This differs from the plain parent's accepted-only
lighting work. Normal words, material/lighting state and original scratch
effects are checked against original execution.

The helper reconstructs camera-space coordinates for projected vertices and
uses raw camera coordinates for vertices flagged behind the near plane. It
rejects orientation as directed by 8c8902fc, clips crossing edges at z=near,
and interpolates lighting factors along those same camera-space edges. It
emits three or four vertex packets, with four-vertex strip order 0,1,3,2.
Packet alpha remains the unchanged material word; RGB is scaled by the input
or interpolated factor. Negative and >1 factors are retained by this helper.
The original output counter, queue cursor and orientation-global effects stay
intact. There are no calls from the helper itself.
