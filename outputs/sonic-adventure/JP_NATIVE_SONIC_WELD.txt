Limits and next work
Fixtures use valid records,real model/action references and a ready task heap.
The player_anim blending-state branch,heap exhaustion,other characters' full
tables,production automatic task/texture lifetimes and event queue transitions
still need qualification. Frame callbacks/camera in the regression capture are
diagnostic. Original Sonic callback/FSM,main loop,stages and gameplay remain
unfinished. Aura/njSimpleAction dependencies also remain. The native numerical
policy is value-only RM=1,DN=1;no FPSCR flags/traps are emulated here.

GCC checkout,LLVM/Windows tools,Python and your own Japanese HDR-0001 v1.007
disc inputs. Choose fresh output directories. Tools refuse proof overwrites.

optional --distribution/--checkout. It reads WSL source/data and verifies the
compressed module against the supplied local track03.bin. It changes neither
preserved source/build tree and publishes no generated game data.

the qualified baseline is work/sonic-adventure/jp-native-event-pose-v3.

--module-inputs --binary --baseline --output. Its baseline is the new runtime
output;--inputs is the existing prepared motion-data input directory.
The verified outputs here are jp-native-player-weld-v1 and
jp-native-sonic-weld-v1 under work/sonic-adventure.
