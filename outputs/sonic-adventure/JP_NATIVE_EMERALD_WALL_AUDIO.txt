Windows wall-clock audio during the Emerald Coast stream

The native sound clock uses the Windows wall clock. The scene pumps
the native wait service during GPU completion polling and at effect
queue updates. It stops accumulation before draining muted WASAPI.

Run verify_native_emerald_wall_audio.py with the verified jump-audio
build and the same local disc, collision and character inputs. The
run passed 825 checks and delivered 675,144 stereo sample frames.
The pickup, jump and landing reached the same update blocks as
before. The audio queue reported backpressure 46 times. Shutdown
accounted for 8,260 unrendered samples as cancelled.

This synchronous research harness verifies real-clock accounting and
delivery. It does not establish low-latency or uninterrupted audible
gameplay. Overall project completion remains about 10%.
