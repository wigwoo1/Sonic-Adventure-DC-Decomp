Native Ninja Simple projection scratch lease (Windows research)

The generated owner extends the verified Simple projection build with
jp_simple_calc_with_records. A successful, visible projection calls a
synchronous consumer while the actual shared vertex pointer and capacity
still refer to the guarded 32-byte Simple records. The consumer receives
the model, the read-only records, the vertex count, and its context.
Use the records only during that call. Return zero on success or a
nonzero status on failure. The original projection-only entry remains
available and calls the same owner without a consumer.

The caller and consumer run on the game thread. Nested projection and
scratch reallocation or shutdown are refused during consumption. The
shared pointer and capacity are restored before returning even when the
consumer fails or a damaged guard is detected. Culled models skip the
consumer and report the original projection result.

Run build_native_simple_lease.py with the simple-calc-v4 baseline, the
verified simple-material-check-v2 directory, and a new work output
directory. Run verify_native_simple_lease.py with that new build.
The five cases cover visible success, consumer error, culling, invalid
span, and guard damage. All 3,341 prior projection checks also pass on
the resulting DLL. Generated source and the combined DLL stay local.
