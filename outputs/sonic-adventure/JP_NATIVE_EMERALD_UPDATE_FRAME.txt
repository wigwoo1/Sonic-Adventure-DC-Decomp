stage and submits the completed frame. It checks that player zero still
owns the task and its work records after the scheduler returns. The
caller owns partial-recording cleanup and presentation.

same local disc, collision and character inputs. The Windows build
compiled, linked and passed 600 checks. The probe delegates its final
player and stage tick to the new entry. It awarded one ring and both
captures matched the prior test byte for byte.
