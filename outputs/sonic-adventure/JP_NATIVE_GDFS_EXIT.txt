What changed
The real filesystem can now transfer terminal shutdown authority to the native
exit service. File operations remain usable after a claim, while independent
configure/init/reinit/shutdown cannot steal that authority. Full-width tokens
never recycle. An uncommitted claim can be cancelled; once cleanup begins, the
same token retains authority across retries and a successful finish is terminal.
Final shutdown drains or cancels pending native I/O, suppresses late callbacks,
retires the original filesystem handler and releases its real file/arenas.

The general exit service now closes GDFS before RTC, device quiescence or an
individually selected WAIT. It closes the final SDK heap after all selected
consumers. Rejected later claims roll back earlier GDFS/RTC/heap claims without
closing those caller-owned services. Unexpected rollback failures retain cleanup
authority instead of silently forgetting a live owner. Existing public struct
sizes and resource-mask meanings remain unchanged.

New resource bit: JP_EXIT_GDFS=131072. JP_EXIT_SUPPORTED=262143.
JP_EXIT_ALL stays 16383; the old device/heap/RTC mask stays 116726.
The device/heap/RTC/GDFS composition uses 247798. An old RTC-only mask does not
implicitly take filesystem ownership. A caller closing a shared wait must first
close or delegate every service that needs it; unselected owners remain its job.
