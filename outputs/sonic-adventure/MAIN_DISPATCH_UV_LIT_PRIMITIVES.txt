Behavior and data contract
The primitive record contains topology at +0, count at +2, index-stream pointer
at +4 and UV-stream pointer at +14 hexadecimal (+20 decimal). Topology masking
selects triangles, quads, strips or the alternate helper. Each UV pair contains
two signed 16-bit values, converted through FPUL/FLOAT and multiplied by the
literal 1/256. Values outside 0..1 and negative values are preserved.

For accepted vertices the code computes the existing lighting formula from the
three normal words, global light vector and base. Each emitted packet is:
  word 0: original vertex/strip-end control
  words 1..3: projected x, y and reciprocal depth from the cache
  words 4..5: signed U/256 and V/256
  word 6: lighting factor
  word 7: the selected queue's existing value, retained unchanged
The eight-word acquisition header separately carries material data. These bodies
do not multiply packet RGB as the earlier non-UV lighting family does, and they
do not save the computed factor into the input cache's +1c field on tested
ordinary paths. UV stream consumption continues across rejected primitives.
Original counters, winding tests, queue movement and retained state are kept.

The clipped body checks common clip flags and stages six signed UV words at
8c890304 before calling its near helper. The source FLOAT/FTRC staging, R7
argument and UV words are independently checked for triangle cases. Alternate
dispatch saves/restores six other-bank FPU registers and loads light/base/scale
parameters before delegation; all bank/stack effects match original execution.

Three lower helpers remain explicit substitutes, not recovered native code:

  8c61394a: plain alternate-strip helper
  8c614f70: clipped alternate-strip helper
Their fixtures alter scratch CPU/FPU state without generating geometry. Missing
