Sonic Adventure Dreamcast Rev A - response renderer research snapshot

This is incomplete research code, not a playable game or a fully qualified
renderer. Overall project completion remains approximately 10%, estimated
from weighted milestones rather than measured recovered-code coverage.

What is included
The original debug-line renderer at 0x8c63ce24 has a complete instruction
listing, Ghidra draft, translated C body and integration with the existing
research pipeline. Its listing contains 684 instructions and 32 conditional
branch outcomes. Generation and successful linking do not qualify its full
behavior. No new routine has been registered or awarded recovery credit.
The previous qualified-routine count of 675 linked entries is unchanged.

Verification performed for this snapshot
- The 273-source Windows research DLL was rebuilt successfully with the existing
  Clang/LLD script, including -Wall -Wextra -Werror.
- All 684 instruction encodings match the local original executable. The
  translated non-delay labels were checked; 40 delay rows are inlined by the
  retained translator. This static check does not independently prove their
  behavior.
- Six Python files and two PowerShell scripts passed syntax/parse checks.
- Three selected original/native comparisons matched whole software state and
  ordered boundaries. Two returned normally. The debug-enabled case executed
  168 of the renderer's 684 rows and stopped at the unresolved dependency
  0x8c64f5e8 on both sides. That matching stop is a limitation, not success at
  rendering the complete line or returning from the complete renderer.
- No game callee output or execution frame was supplied by these checks.

Remaining work
Recover and verify the dependency at 0x8c64f5e8, then expand response/renderer
coverage and qualify the remaining outcomes. The response helper at 0x8c072946,
movement owner and movement routing still need full qualification. This snapshot
does not change those earlier limitations or claim physical hardware accuracy.

Files to inspect
analysis/player_response_draw_snapshot_checks.json records the exact smoke
cases, software stops and source/build hashes. The translation and connection
reports describe their separate generation/integration scope. Historical reports
are preserved unchanged; their counts describe their own snapshots.

Reproduction (from a workspace with the documented tools and matching retail input)
powershell -File outputs/sonic-adventure/build_player_response_draw.ps1
python -B outputs/sonic-adventure/check_player_response_draw_snapshot.py
The check preserves its historical report and refuses to overwrite it. To rerun,
use a separate checkout without the report or an additive revision. Retail
inputs and assets are local-only and are not distributed with the repository.

Research builds
player_response_draw.dll and player_response_draw.lib are test artifacts, not
a game executable. The release archive preserves previous research builds and
adds these source-matched files, with per-file hashes and a SHA256 manifest.
The decompilation goal remains active.
