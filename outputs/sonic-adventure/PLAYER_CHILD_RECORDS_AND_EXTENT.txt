Why this matters

The original player constructor leaves actor+0x38 unset. Original 8c02f4b0
allocates a collision header and a copy of its shape records, binds its owner,
and installs that collision pointer. Executing this original setup lets the
actual child's stored update callback run through its geometry dependency and
return normally. The test no longer depends on an authored actor collision
pointer for these allocated-child lifecycles. Shape templates and host phase
order are still declared test inputs, not proof of complete retail startup.
