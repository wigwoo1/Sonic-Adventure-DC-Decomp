header words 0..3 and the four words addressed by incoming R4, in normal final
packet order. The code reads/stores in its original reverse order. Three-word
variants retain the selected queue's other five words; they are not zero-filled.
All paths transfer a complete 32-byte queue with PREF. QACR0/QACR1 receive
(cursor >> 24) & 1c; saved high address bits are cursor & fc000000.
The published finalization slot points to the active cursor word, header+16 or
header+20. Acquisition returns the next queue cursor in R0 but does not update
that header cursor until native finish 8c6069a4 runs. The eight-word variants
preserve incoming R4 and also return the queue cursor in R5. The secondary
three-word variant returns it in R4. Other register effects remain original.
