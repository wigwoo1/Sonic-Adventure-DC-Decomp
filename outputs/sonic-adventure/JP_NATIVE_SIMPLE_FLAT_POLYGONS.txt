Native Ninja Simple flat-lit quads and N-gons

The bounded dispatcher now accepts flat-lit quads and N-gons in color
and textured formats. The original drawers retain a single face shade
through near-plane clipping. The port follows the pinned GCC source's
repair for clipped N-gons, which derives the shade from that polygon's
own first three corners.

Build with build_native_simple_flat_polygons.py against the pinned
checkout and jp-native-simple-smooth-polygons-v3. Run
verify_native_simple_flat_polygons.py. Fifteen cases verify defined
packet words, clipping, and rejected spans. Fifteen smooth polygon
cases, four flat textured triangle cases, and all 3,341 projection
comparisons also passed.

GPU presentation and environment-mapped quads and N-gons remain open.
Whole-project completion remains about 10%.
