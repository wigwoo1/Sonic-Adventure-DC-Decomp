Observed initializer behavior
R4 identifies the incoming task-like record. It reads work through +20 hex and
an entity-like record through +24. It clears work state bytes/halfwords and two
work words, clears three entity words at +7c/+80/+84, and copies original
appearance position words through the entity's +ec pointer into +dc/+e0/+e4.
It then calls seven initialization services and the native model selector in
original order, supplying work in R4 and entity in R5 each time.

Two subordinate calls are now native:
- Existing 0c90d210 returns without other state changes. It is reused from
  source/retail_return_delay_stubs.c, not registered as a new routine.
- New 0c909d40 clears the entity halfword at +812 and writes ffff to +816,
  retaining neighboring halfwords. Its caller supplies the entity in R5.

The selector uses the previously established bit priority 10,08,04,02,01 hex,
installs the chosen original model pointer, and sets the three scale words to
3e999999. Bit 10 selects the inventoried AL_MAIN face-strip model. The bridge
uses the exact existing CPU layouts when calling the selector and no-op helper.

After subordinate initialization, the caller clears bits 0e from entity+60,
clears bit 0 from the appearance halfword at +36, clears entity+74 and the low
halfword at +78, and writes original constant 3e4ccccc (approximately 0.2) to
entity+d8. It chooses a tail call by appearance flags in priority 40,04,08 hex:
0c90fb82, 0c90fca0 or 0c90fd42. With none set, it returns directly. Tail paths
restore the incoming PR and saved registers before transferring to the service;
the restored PR is preserved rather than replaced by a new call return address.

Raw wide FMOV behavior remains original. The final eight-byte literal store at
entity+d8 also writes companion word 09000900 into +dc, overwriting the earlier
copied position word. Selector wide stores retain their original overlapping
scale writes. Tests preserve these effects rather than repairing them; wide
fixtures do not establish the game's normal entry mode.
