Observed behavior
The material loop selects this pair for layout 1200000 hex. The segment's +14 hex
field points to signed 16-bit U/V pairs. Values are converted to floats and scaled
by 1/256. The stream advances across faces and rejected inputs as in the original.
Accepted ordinary faces sum their vertex normals and call recovered numerical
normalizer 8c63a94c. Lighting is base minus the normalized normal/light dot product.

Triangle packets contain control, projected x/y/reciprocal depth, U/V, a lighting
factor word and a retained eighth word. The first two triangle vertices retain
their selected queue's previous factor word; the final vertex receives the new
face factor. Input factor caches remain unchanged. These packets carry a factor,
not the material RGBA layout of the earlier untextured face family.
