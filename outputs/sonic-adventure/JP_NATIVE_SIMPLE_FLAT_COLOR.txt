Native Ninja Simple flat-lit untextured triangles

The bounded dispatcher now uses the pinned original flat-lit triangle
drawers for untextured materials. They normalize the sum of the corner
normals and compute one material color for the face. The near-plane
drawer keeps that color for the resulting triangle or quad. The queue
uses flat shading and preserves material alpha.

Build with build_native_simple_flat_color.py using the pinned checkout
and jp-native-simple-smooth-color-strips-v2, then run
verify_native_simple_flat_color.py. Six cases verify the defined packet
fields, including the final face colors and clipped topology. The
original drawer leaves the color words of the first two vertices
untouched. Five strip and two mixed-model regression cases passed,
along with 3,341 original projection checks.

Verification covers CPU queue output. GPU presentation and direct
SH-4 drawer comparison remain open. Generated code and the combined
DLL stay local. Whole-project completion remains about 10%.
