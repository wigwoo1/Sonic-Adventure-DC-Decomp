Raw clock input and original behavior
rtc_sample_bus.c wraps the existing driver backend. It consumes borrowed finite
32-bit words in exact read order at physical 00710000/00710004 through P0/P1/P2
aliases. The game reads two words and combines their low 16 bits. Full raw words
are retained for diagnostic logging. There is no OS-clock query, calendar
conversion, automatic increment, stable-value generator or fabricated return.

Unconfigured input stops -2. Configured empty/exhausted input stops -4. Wrong
width/null output stops -3 without consuming a sample or changing the output.
The six alias/address combinations, full-width values, borrowed-input immutability,
reset and invalid-configuration behavior pass direct checks. Writes to these two
words are unsupported in this model. Other accesses retain previous routes.
These rules describe this software test backend, not an electrical specification
or a measurement of the user's Dreamcast.

The original diagnostic sink writes only when shared clock/diagnostic flags have
bit 8 set and its cursor is below its limit. Tests check disabled/full/one-slot/
two-slot buffers, cursor boundaries and invalid storage, including guard bytes.
Clock update bits 1 and 2 share that same flag word: all four combinations are
tested while preserving diagnostic bit 8. The consistency helper accepts two
matching readings and otherwise retains the supplied earlier timestamp. The
stable loop compares against its initial sample until three subsequent readings
match; changing and exhausted streams retain real retry/stop behavior.

Conditional-unreachability evidence
Read analysis/movie_rtc_qualification.json for checked instructions and addresses.
Every selected instruction's bytes are checked against the original input, with
critical instruction checks and the transfer's normal epilogue/entry targets.
The proof assumes immutable code/literals, no asynchronous RAM mutation, normal
original entry/return ABI, valid mapped stack scratch, and disabled diagnostic
storage or storage separate from active frames, control globals and code.

The synchronized transfer's sole normal RTS is preceded by mov #0,r0; its
subsequent restores do not change R0. The RTC read likewise assigns R0=0 after
logging. Host faults stop execution before a caller resumes; they are not guest
error returns. Thus the callers' guest-error arms cannot execute in this domain.
The stable reader's first successful call sets its preserved flag to one, ruling
out its initial failure retry/fallback. In the two-sample comparator, the relevant
scratch pointers are exactly four bytes apart, then equal after one increment;
two unsigned-loop outcomes cannot execute. Valid mapped scratch avoids wrap.

This is a conditional proof for the declared input/ABI model, not a claim covering
arbitrary self-aliasing buffer corruption, modified code or asynchronous mutation.
The native C retains every original branch/error arm. No outcome is manufactured
merely to increase recovery counts. The existing transfer adapter covers 101/148
rows in this suite; its earlier canonical qualification is preserved, not replaced.
