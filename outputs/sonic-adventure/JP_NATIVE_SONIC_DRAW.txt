Original Sonic player draw callback on Windows

The build extracts the callback from the pinned Japanese source and
connects it to the native action renderer, retained texture lists, Sonic
toe owner, and scoped aura draw. The fixture supplies a task and player
record and samples frame 8.5 of the original updraft action.

The hidden callback emitted zero batches. The visible uncharged
callback emitted 53 body batches. The charged callback emitted 113
batches, with 60 additional aura batches. The 512 by 512 GPU fixture
presented the charged frame twice. Texture Off rendered a distinct
vertex and material colour image with zero texture and sampler
bindings and zero texture uploads.

The callback fixture passed 453 checks and six Simple SDK regression
cases passed. Each new recording reserves its model packet buffers
and retains the required texture surfaces before calling the renderer.

Build with build_native_sonic_draw.py against the pinned checkout and
the qualified aura build. Run verify_native_sonic_draw_frames.py with
the output build, local character inputs, and Japanese track 03.
Retail inputs, generated original code, combined binaries, and
screenshots stay local.

This is a prepared callback test. Live player-task scheduling and the
super, event, blend, and afterimage branches remain unverified here.
Overall project completion is about 10%.
