Reproduction

Follow JP_NATIVE_DRAW_CONTROLS.txt and JP_NATIVE_ACTION_RUNTIME.txt prerequisites.

   --output; verify_native_morph_math.py --workspace --build --upstream --binary
   --numeric-dll --output.

   verify_native_model_morph.py with the same comparison arguments as above.
3. build_native_toe_models.py --workspace --upstream --inputs(original prepared
   motion/model inputs) --baseline(morph) --output; verify_native_toe_models.py
   with the comparison arguments.
4. build_native_sonic_toes.py --workspace --upstream --baseline(toe data) --output.
5. build_native_sonic_run_data.py --workspace --upstream --inputs --baseline
   (toe control) --output. The remaining current-build verifiers use this build.

   arguments; the pose verifier also takes --motion-inputs.

   textures) --track(JP track03.bin) --output. Re-run math and original game-init
