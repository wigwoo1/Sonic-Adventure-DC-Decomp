Scheduled Emerald Coast pickup sparkles on Windows

The Windows ring draw callback uses the original sparkle frame and
transform loop. The fixture reads the eight animation descriptors from
the verified Japanese executable and loads the eight original texture
payloads from EFF_REGULAR.PRS into a local PVM list. The renderer borrows
the list during submission and restores the matrix and texture list.

Build with build_native_emerald_ring_sparkle.py against the pinned
checkout and ring completion build. Run verify_native_emerald_ring_sparkle.py
with the same local disc and scene inputs. At the sixth sparkle tick,
three sprite calls produced two nonempty texture batches. Drawing left
the task work byte-for-byte unchanged and the fifteen-tick lifetime
still completed through the scheduler.

The capture layers pre-pickup and later sparkle submissions into one
diagnostic frame. It uses the current native sprite blend state and
fixture camera. Stage-owned sprite assets, pickup audio, and the full
stage loop remain open. Overall completion is about 10%.
