Observed behavior
Although selected by the face-lit parents for topology c000, ordinary output
lights each vertex separately. Other-bank light/material parameters feed FIPR;
the factor is lighting base minus the normal/light dot product. Alpha stays
unchanged; each material RGB component is multiplied by that factor without
clamping. Packets contain control, projected x/y/reciprocal depth and RGBA.
The factor is also written at vertex+1c and an OCBWB event is issued for it.
This preserves the original ordinary-path behavior, rather than imposing one
uniform face colour on the whole strip.

For accepted near crossings, the clipped helper sums the three current normals,
calls recovered numerical normalizer 8c63a94c, calculates one face RGBA block,
then calls recovered face-colour clipper 8c6178c6 with orientation disabled.
The clipper preserves early queue colour words and supplies the new colour at
the provoking vertices. Clipped four-vertex geometry uses strip order 0,1,3,2.
Flip bits, alternating winding, strict orientation rejection, grouping, counters
and cursor advancement are preserved. Zero-area triangles are rejected.
Both new helpers call existing empty stub 8c611708. The clipped body additionally
calls the native normalizer and clipper. These three dependencies receive zero
new routine credit; only the two new alternate entries are registered.
