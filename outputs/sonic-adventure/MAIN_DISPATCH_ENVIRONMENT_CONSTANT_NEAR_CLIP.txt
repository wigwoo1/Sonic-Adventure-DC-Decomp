Observed behavior
R4/R5/R6 identify three cached vertices. The helper generates UVs from their normals
and the matrix published through 8c890238. It computes 128 + 128 * dot(normal,
matrix column), truncates to integer and stores/re-reads the signed low 16 bits.
It reconstructs camera coordinates where required, rejects orientation where
requested and clips against the near plane. Intersections interpolate the integer
UVs and quantize again before final 1/256 scaling.

Initial UV words occupy stack-local +14 hex. In ordinary single precision, the
frame contains six saved GPRs, one four-byte FR12 save and 50 hex local bytes.
Geometry scratch is 8c890278; output UV scratch is 8c890268; orientation state is
8c8902fc. The input vertex cache remains unchanged. No game calls or lighting
normalizer occur inside this helper.

Packets contain control, projected x/y/reciprocal depth, U/V, factor 1.0 and an
untouched final word. Every emitted vertex gets factor 1.0, including the first
two. This constant is set internally, rather than supplied through FR4 as in the
earlier face-lighting clipper. Quadrilaterals emit in strip order 0,1,3,2; the final
packet carries control f0000000.
