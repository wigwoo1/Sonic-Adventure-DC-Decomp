What closed the ordinary scalar acceptance gate
The routine has 157 original instruction rows and 24 conditional outcomes.
Fresh execution covers 154 rows and 23 outcomes. The remaining three rows at
074FD6/074FD8/074FDA follow the 074FD4 upper-half-turn branch. The new analysis
accounts for those rows and that outcome within the profile below. Every
original row and branch remains in source, including this branch.

The original caller forms a nonnegative horizontal magnitude, then calls its
actual atan2 closure. The analysis binds the original bytes, scalar operations,
constant pointers, polynomial coefficients, callee register preservation and
selected formulas. It leaves the classifier's integer result unconstrained and
bounds every possible selected formula and its fallback.

Two cases call the original power-of-two scaling helper 10E7B8 with -2/-1;
they do not call the polynomial helper. Its 256 symbolic exponent cases cover
all fraction bits for nonnegative inputs in [0,1]. The other helper is the real
five-FMAC polynomial at 10FB94. Exact rational bounds include its coefficient
error and rounded operations; no ideal atan implementation is substituted.

The final finite angle-times-scale interval is
[-6143857/268435456, 8388617/256], approximately
[-0.02288765087723732, 32768.03515625]. It is strictly above -1 and below 32769.
Original truncation toward zero therefore yields an integer from 0 to 32768.
The original FFFF mask leaves that range unchanged, so the signed comparison
cannot exceed 8000. Original NaN handling either stops or converts to a value
whose masked phase is zero. Infinite ratios take finite angle exits.

Scope and limits
This is a reproducible source-bound arithmetic analysis for normal entry,
fixed original code/math literals/coefficient table, an ordinary RAM stack
that does not alias those fixed inputs, PR=0/SZ=0 and completed RM0/RM1 scalar
arithmetic, without asynchronous foreign writes or mode changes. It is not a
universal physical SH-4 proof or qualification of altered math data/precision.
The broader 074FD4->074FD6 obligation remains explicitly open. Existing
074626/6402C0 precision obligations, world/collision branches, 0730D8 parent,
physical exception/MMU/device behavior and complete gameplay remain unfinished.
