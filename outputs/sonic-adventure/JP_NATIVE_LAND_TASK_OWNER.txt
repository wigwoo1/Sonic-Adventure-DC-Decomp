Persistent Emerald Coast land task on Windows

The static land owner registers one native scheduler task and borrows
the decoded entries and texture list until close. Each frame supplies
its camera, block mask, visibility and step. The frame scope restores
the caller's land, camera and step state. The destructor retires the
task's borrowed references.

The fixture dispatched three visible frames through the same task.
Hidden land and later steps skipped model submissions. Duplicate opens,
reentrant frames and close during drawing were rejected. An unscoped
scheduler draw skipped the task, and a fresh owner opened after close.
The last frame produced the same GPU captures as the direct land
fixture, with 465 checks passed.

Run build_native_land_task_owner.py against the qualified land dispatch
build, then verify_native_emerald_land_owner.py with the local Japanese
inputs. The test still provides the stage resources and camera. The
live stage loader and moving land are separate work. Overall project
completion is about 10%.
