Emerald Coast stream timing profile

The verifier measures native wall counts around GPU submission,
completion polling, window copy, presentation, readback and audio
service. It also measures the gaps between presentation callbacks.
Audio-service measurements can overlap GPU-wait measurements.

Run verify_native_emerald_stream_profile.py with the verified
jump-audio build and the same local disc, collision and character
inputs. The run passed 852 checks. The 47 callback gaps totalled
13.95 seconds out of 16.14 seconds of scene audio clock time. The
longest gap was about 574 ms. GPU submission totalled 1.10 seconds
and six screenshot readbacks totalled about 4 ms.

The gaps include native simulation, packet recording and other
work between callbacks. These measurements identify where to
subdivide the profile. Overall completion remains about 10%.
