What the family does
The entry word selects triangles (0000), quads (4000), strips (8000), or the
alternate-strip helper (c000), after masking the two high bits of a 16-bit word.
The first body performs screen-space orientation rejection; the second also
checks per-vertex clipping masks and delegates near-plane triangles when needed.
The recovered bodies now run directly inside the native mesh/material chain.

For accepted vertices, the code reads a three-component normal from cache words
0..2 and computes, in successive binary32 operations:
  factor = ((base - normal.x * light.x) - normal.y * light.y)
           - normal.z * light.z
Light components occupy 8c890248/24c/250; base is at 8c890258. The factor is
written to the vertex cache at +1c. A packet contains control, projected x/y/depth,
the unchanged material word at +10 (inferred alpha), and the other three material
words multiplied by the factor. Negative and greater-than-one factors are
preserved by these bodies; no artificial clamp has been introduced. Packet
controls, queue advancement, original counters including wraparound, and cache
writes retain original behavior.

Alternate dispatch saves/restores ten registers in the other floating-point
bank around its helper, loading lighting/material values into that bank. The
complete body and service boundary effects are compared; original FPU bank/width
handling and inherited numerical guards remain in place.

Three lower helpers are still explicit substitutes, not recovered native code:
  8c612e64 — near-plane lighting helper, called at four sites
  8c6136c0 — alternate strip helper for the first body
  8c614bb0 — alternate strip helper for the clipped body
Their test effects alter scratch registers/FPU values without producing geometry.
Missing-helper and failing-helper partial state is checked for every target.
Fourteen other primitive emitters also remain unfinished, making 17 remaining
primitive/lower service targets. This increase from 16 reflects discovery of the
three lower dependencies while replacing the two parent substitutes.

Connected mesh coverage
All 48 constructed meshes invoke the plain new body after actual material
setup, filtering and vertex projection with normals. Both FPU banks, both
orientation signs and primary/secondary three-word acquisition are covered.
Thirty-two meshes independently verify final vertex packet colours/cache/cursor.
The other sixteen place an input behind the near plane while ENV bit 2 bypasses
classification, so they also select the plain body. These meshes do not call the
near helper; the clipped body and explicit lower-helper routing were covered by
direct parent cases. This coverage clarification was discovered while preparing
the later MAIN_DISPATCH_LIT_NEAR_CLIP.txt checkpoint, which clears that bypass
bit and explicitly verifies clipped-mesh helper calls and interpolated output.
With otherwise zero material flags, bit 100000 changes the acquisition cursor;
bit 200000 also selects a different primitive layout. The latter is not claimed
as native coverage for this family. Earlier acquisition tests remain separate.
