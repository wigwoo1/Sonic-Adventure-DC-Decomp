What the original code does
The decrement helper stores argument minus one through its original literal.
The three setters load the existing driver pointer at 8c67c004 and call the
original installer with indices zero, five or eight. Index eight supplies a
zero argument. The installer saves the interrupt mask, masks interrupts in
the modeled SR, and stores callback/argument words in the selected slot. A null
callback invokes the actual original reset selector. The old interrupt mask is
restored. These operations establish software CPU/RAM state; no physical IRQ
delivery or callback execution follows merely from storing an address.
