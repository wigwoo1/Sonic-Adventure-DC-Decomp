Observed behavior
R4/R5/R6 identify three cached vertices. Active FR4 supplies a face lighting factor.
UVs are generated from each cached normal and the matrix published at 8c890238.
The helper computes 128 + 128 * dot(normal, matrix column), truncates each value
to integer and stores/re-reads its signed low 16 bits. It then reconstructs camera
coordinates where required and clips against the near plane. Intersections
interpolate those integer UVs and quantize again before final 1/256 scaling.
This is different from directly interpolating floating-point environment UVs.

The helper stores its initial six UV words at stack-local +14 hex, before
orientation rejection. Geometry scratch is 8c890278; output UV scratch is
8c890268; orientation state is 8c8902fc. In ordinary single precision, its frame
uses six saved GPRs, one four-byte FR12 save and 50 hex local bytes.
Input vertex caches remain unchanged. No game calls or lighting normalizer occur
inside this helper. The connected parent supplies the previously calculated
normalized face-lighting factor.

Packets contain control, projected x/y/reciprocal depth, U/V, factor and retained
padding. The first two vertices retain their selected queue's prior factor;
the third and, for quadrilaterals, fourth copy active FR4 exactly. The final word
is untouched. Quadrilaterals emit in strip order 0,1,3,2; the final packet carries
control f0000000.
