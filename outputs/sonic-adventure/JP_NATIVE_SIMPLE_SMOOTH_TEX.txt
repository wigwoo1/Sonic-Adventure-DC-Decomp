Native Ninja Simple smooth-lit textured triangles (Windows research)

The pinned original textured triangle drawer now uses the existing
first-light setup, projection lease and retained texture queue. It
writes per-vertex light intensity and signed texture coordinates.
For a triangle crossing the near plane, it interpolates intensity
and texture coordinates separately, then truncates the interpolated
UVs with the existing SH-4 conversion service. The material color and
alpha remain in the original textured intensity header.

Build with build_native_simple_smooth_tex.py against the pinned
Japanese checkout and jp-native-simple-smooth-clip-v1, then run
verify_native_simple_smooth_tex.py. Four complete packet comparisons
cover an unclipped triangle, two clipping topologies and a culled
backface. All four submitted frames retain their texture after release.
Six smooth color and three unlit texture clipping regressions passed,
along with all 3,341 original projection checks.

Scope is a single smooth-lit textured triangle meshset. No direct
original SH-4 drawer comparison or GPU presentation was performed.
Generated source and the combined DLL stay local. Smooth-lit strips,
flat and environment-mapped materials, and complete model dispatch
remain open. Overall project estimate: approximately 10%.
