Native Ninja Simple material stage (Windows research)

The pinned Japanese SDK's per-material setup in _njSimpleDrawModelSub
now compiles as a callable native stage. It updates the caller's light
material and returns the effective flags, drawer mask, texture number,
color mode and queue start request, in that order. An untextured material
returns 0xffffffff as its texture number. The queue request values are
1 textured opaque, 2 textured transparent, 3 untextured opaque, and
4 untextured transparent. The caller supplies valid material, light and
five-word output buffers. No texture is bound and no geometry is submitted.

Use build_native_simple_material.py with the verified jp-native-simple-calc-v4
baseline and pinned upstream checkout. Then use verify_native_simple_material.py
with that baseline and the resulting build directory. Use new directories
under work/sonic-adventure for each run. Generated upstream C and the combined
DLL remain local.

Verification: 8,192 native cases compared every output word with an independent
exact-rational rounding oracle and a dispatch truth table. The cases cover
all combinations of texture, alpha, environment, flat and unlit flags, four
control bits, two attribute masks and eight color samples. The linked DLL also
passed all 3,341 previous comparisons against original projection execution.
The material stage itself has not yet been compared against SH-4 execution.

Next: consume the returned requests through the real queue provider, maintain
the projection scratch lease through mesh emission, and recover the original
drawer bodies. Full model drawing, gameplay, English localization and later
platforms are not verified. Whole project remains approximately 10%.
