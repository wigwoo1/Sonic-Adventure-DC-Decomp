What the caller contract means
The child reports failure for invalid collision structures or unsupported shape
types and leaves the caller-owned direction output unchanged. The larger
0x8c072946 caller does not test that result before reading and copying the three
output words. Its actual local frame is 0x88 bytes, and the output occupies its
final 12 local bytes at offset 0x60, below the saved registers.

One real successful call writes a negative-X direction into that local output.
A second call carries the completed RAM/CPU/FPU forward, changes only ordinary
call and collision/motion inputs, and supplies null collision information. The
child rejects it, but the caller consumes the prior call's direction and can
return normally. A cold case with zero backing bytes instead reaches the
existing numerical stop. No private stack byte is seeded or repaired.

This is evidence about original software behavior, not proof that ordinary
retail gameplay supplies such invalid structures. The decompilation preserves
the unchecked read. It must not silently initialize the output or invent a
successful child result. The retail collision-flag/info validity invariant,
allocation/stack alias exclusions and broader response numerical domain still
need evidence. The larger response routine remains unregistered.
