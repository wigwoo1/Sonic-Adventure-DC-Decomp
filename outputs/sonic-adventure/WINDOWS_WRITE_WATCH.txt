Method and contract
The tool allocates private Windows memory with write tracking. Reference and
native regions first compare completely. At every recorded ordinary boundary,
it captures reference changed-page bytes, applies those deltas to an expected
image and compares every page reported changed by either execution. Other pages
retain equality by induction. Final bytes compare completely again. Unaligned
VRAM embedded inside the native software-device structure maps conservatively
onto logical pages; extra reported pages are compared too. First-touch demand-
zero reads were observed to produce extra reported pages on this host.
No guest checkpoint, cached execution, skipped instruction/callee, sampled
ordinary boundary or supplied game-helper return replaces original execution.
The established separately decoded full-VRAM-clear loop and its fingerprints/
invariants remain unchanged; this is not a stronger claim about that compressed
trace. The OS page-tracking contract and single-thread ownership are explicit
assumptions. Exported buffers retain allocation ownership and prevent explicit
close; borrowed raw native addresses must not outlive their owner.
