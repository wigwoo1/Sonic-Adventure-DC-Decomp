Native Ninja Simple smooth-lit untextured strips

The bounded model dispatcher now routes smooth-lit untextured strips
through the original strip drawers. Regular vertices use the SH-4 dot
product value service. The near-plane path interpolates intensity at the
intersection and applies it to material RGB while preserving alpha.

Run build_native_simple_smooth_color_strips.py against the pinned Japanese
checkout and jp-native-simple-model-v1, then run
verify_native_simple_smooth_color_strips.py on the resulting build.
Five cases cover continuous, culled and clipped strips and invalid
geometry spans. The mixed model regression and all 3,341 original
projection comparisons also passed.

Verification covers native queue bytes. Direct comparison with the
original SH-4 strip execution and GPU presentation remain open.
Generated upstream source and the combined DLL stay local. Overall
project estimate remains about 10%.
