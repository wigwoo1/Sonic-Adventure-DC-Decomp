Sonic movement over the original Emerald Coast floor

The Windows scheduler fixture places Sonic at the original Emerald
Coast start, finds the floor in the locally decoded Japanese stage
geometry, and samples that floor after each movement tick. It checks
that the grounded position reaches both the original movement history
and the scheduled draw callback.

original textures and with Texture Off. The camera is translated to
the start for this character capture.
