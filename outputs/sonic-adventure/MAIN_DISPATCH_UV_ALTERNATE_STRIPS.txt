Behavior
The helpers handle alternate strips selected by topology c000. They consume
signed 16-bit U/V pairs for each strip corner, including rejected triangles,
and scale them by 1/256. UV consumption continues across successive strips,
even where vertex indices restart. Strip flip and alternating winding control
orientation rejection; zero-area triangles are rejected.

The lighting factor is base minus the normal/light dot product. Other-bank FPU
parameters and FIPR effects are preserved. Packets contain control, projected
x/y/reciprocal depth, U/V and the lighting factor. The eighth queue word retains
its previous value. Ordinary emission leaves the vertex-cache factor word
unchanged and issues no OCBWB write-back event.

The clipped helper first checks common rejection flags and near-plane crossings.
After orientation acceptance, it disables repeated orientation testing, stages
the raw signed UVs at 8c890304 and calls recovered UV clipper 8c611a14. That helper
interpolates geometry and lighting, truncates interpolated UVs back to signed
16-bit coordinates, then scales by 1/256. Four-vertex output uses order 0,1,3,2.
Both new bodies reuse the existing empty stub at 8c611708 without new recovery
credit. The clipped body also reuses the native UV clipper without new credit.
