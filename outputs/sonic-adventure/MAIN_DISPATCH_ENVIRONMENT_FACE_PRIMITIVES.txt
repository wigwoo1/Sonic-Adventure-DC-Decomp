Observed behavior
The material loop selects this pair for layout 1600000 hex, with secondary-header
selection handled separately. UVs are generated from each cached vertex normal
and the matrix published through 8c890238. In supported ordinary precision:
  U = 0.5 + 0.5 * dot(normal, matrix column 0)
  V = 0.5 + 0.5 * dot(normal, matrix column 1)
The original multiply/FMAC order is retained. Segment UV tables are not used.

Accepted faces sum three vertex normals and use existing native normalizer
8c63a94c. Their lighting factor is ambient minus the normalized normal/light dot
product. Triangle packets contain control, projected x/y/reciprocal depth, U/V,
a lighting-factor word and an untouched eighth word. The first two vertices
retain their selected queue's earlier factor; the third receives the face factor.
Direct input caches remain unchanged.

Near crossings stage vertex pointers and the face factor in active FR4 to lower
helper 8c6173ba. Alternate topology c000 delegates to 8c614580 / 8c616474 after
the original matrix/light/bank preparation. All three lower helpers remain
explicit fixtures in this checkpoint. Their output beyond those boundaries is
not yet proved. The numerical normalizer is reused without new routine credit.
