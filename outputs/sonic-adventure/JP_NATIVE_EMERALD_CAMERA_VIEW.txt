Emerald Coast POINT camera rendering on Windows

The native Sonic draw now uses the selected POINT mode's unobstructed
pose. The camera targets Sonic 7.5 units above his feet and sits the
authored distance away along the direction from the region target.
The native Ninja view routine builds the matrix in a temporary stack.
The adapter restores the previous view and matrix stack after setup.

Build with build_native_emerald_camera_view.py against the camera-mode
and stage-tick builds. Run verify_native_emerald_camera_view.py with the
same local inputs. It verifies that the eye maps to the view origin,
that Sonic's target maps to the authored depth, and that the surrounding
renderer state is restored.

The capture uses POINT for Sonic and the existing diagnostic view for
land and ring overlays. The full scene still needs a shared camera.
Camera smoothing, wall sweep, orbit controls and roll remain open.
Overall completion is about 10%.
